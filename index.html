01 • Problem

The stewardship data problem.

Hospitals already generate patient, antimicrobial and microbiology data. The challenge is converting fragmented information into timely, reviewable stewardship action.

Antimicrobial complexity

Multiple antibiotics, wards, diagnoses, doses and durations require continuous review.

Microbiology fragmentation

Culture and susceptibility information must be connected with the therapy actually being used.

Limited stewardship capacity

Clinical pharmacists and physicians cannot manually review every potential opportunity.

AMR pressure

Delayed or inappropriate antimicrobial use can contribute to antimicrobial resistance.

02 • Solution

The stewardship operating layer.

AMSP is designed as the intelligence and workflow layer between hospital data and stewardship action.

04 • Clinical intelligence

Start narrow. Measure everything.

Initial use cases are intentionally reviewable rather than autonomous.

De-escalation review

Culture/susceptibility available → review whether narrower therapy is appropriate.

Duration review

Identify antimicrobial courses approaching a defined stewardship review point.

Renal-dose review

Surface cases where relevant renal-function data warrants pharmacist review.

Duplicate therapy

Identify overlapping antimicrobial coverage requiring clinical review.

Restricted antibiotic review

Prioritize selected high-priority agents according to hospital policy.

Microbiology follow-up

Surface culture results requiring stewardship attention.

06 • Safety & governance

Built to earn clinical trust.

The prototype demonstrates governance principles; it is not a clinically deployed medical device.

Human-in-the-loop

AMSP supports review. Qualified clinicians retain decision authority.

Explainability

Recommendation context, evidence and relevant data timestamps should be visible to reviewers.

Auditability

Clinical review actions should be attributable, time-stamped and versioned.

Role-based access

Admin, pharmacist, physician, microbiologist and auditor roles can have distinct permissions.

Synthetic demo data

This public prototype uses fictional patient information only.

Interoperability-ready

Future implementation can map hospital data to established healthcare exchange models.

07 • Standards & regulatory roadmap

Designed around clinical, privacy and software-safety frameworks.

AMSP is a prototype. We are planning the product architecture and validation program around the following guidance and standards. Alignment or compliance will only be claimed after the applicable requirements are formally assessed and implemented.

🇮🇳 ICMR Antimicrobial Stewardship Program Guideline

Primary Indian clinical stewardship reference. AMSP workflows will incorporate evidence-based treatment guidance, local microbiology/resistance patterns, antibiograms, antimicrobial policy, surveillance and antibiotic-consumption data.

🇮🇳 ICMR antimicrobial guidance

Planned references include ICMR Treatment Guidelines for Antimicrobial Use in Common Syndromes, guidance for carbapenem-resistant Gram-negative infections, and relevant AMR/AST guidance.

🌍 WHO AWaRe

Use the Access, Watch and Reserve framework and related stewardship indicators to support antimicrobial-use monitoring and stewardship analytics.

🔐 India: DPDP + ABDM

Privacy-by-design, data minimization, appropriate consent/processing controls, security safeguards and health-data governance will be incorporated for Indian deployment, subject to applicable law and implementation requirements.

🔗 HL7 FHIR

Design the interoperability layer around established healthcare data-exchange resources so future EHR, LIS, medication and laboratory integrations can be mapped consistently.

⚕️ ISO 14971

Planned risk-management framework for identifying hazards, evaluating clinical/software risks, implementing controls and monitoring residual risk across the product lifecycle.

💻 IEC 62304

Planned software lifecycle framework if AMSP functionality falls within medical-device software scope, covering development, maintenance and controlled software processes.

🛡️ IEC 81001-5-1

Planned health-software cybersecurity lifecycle approach covering security activities throughout development and maintenance.

🏥 ISO 13485

Potential quality-management framework if AMSP is determined to be medical-device software requiring a medical-device QMS.

🇺🇸 FDA CDS framework

For any future U.S. deployment, intended use and functionality will be assessed against the FDA's current Clinical Decision Support Software guidance and applicable digital-health requirements.

🇪🇺 EU MDR / MDCG 2019-11 Rev.1

For future EU deployment, software qualification and classification will be assessed under the EU medical-device framework and current MDCG software guidance.

🧪 Clinical validation & governance

Retrospective validation → shadow mode → governed pilot, with clinical ownership, versioned evidence, audit trails, human review and predefined safety/utility metrics.

IMPORTANT

These are the planned reference frameworks—not a claim of current certification, regulatory approval or clinical compliance. Final applicability depends on AMSP's intended purpose, functionality, jurisdiction, deployment model and risk classification.
